题目：面向地产项目团队的信息协同型AI智能体框架设计
高海涛
（蓝城伟业控股有限公司，浙江 杭州，310000）
【中图分类号】TU71；TP182      【文献标识码】：A    

【摘要】随着大语言模型与AI Agent技术快速发展，人工智能正从单点任务辅助向复杂业务协同演进。但在地产领域，现有AI应用多局限于面向个人、单任务的工具形态，缺乏适配地产行业协同工作模式与多源数据特征的团队级框架。针对这一缺口，本文提出一种面向地产项目团队的信息协同型AI智能体框架：它以自然语言为统一交互入口，将项目态势、组织规则与业务能力组织为团队共享、随项目推进持续更新的记忆中枢，支撑团队成员的信息协同与受控执行。该框架已通过实际业务案例完成落地验证，能够有效缓解企业存量项目管理资料浩瀚而难以活化、项目经验以人为节点并随人员变动而流失的困境，为地产及相关行业垂直领域团队协作AI的落地与系统框架搭建提供借鉴。
【关键词】智能体；信息协同；项目管理；知识管理；大语言模型；地产项目

Framework Design of Information Collaborative AI Agent for Real Estate Project Teams
GAO Haitao
(Weiye Buletown Holdings Co., Ltd., Hangzhou, Zhejiang 310000, China)
【Abstract】With the rapid advancement of large language models and AI Agent technologies, artificial intelligence is evolving from single-point task assistance toward complex business collaboration. However, in the real estate sector, existing AI applications remain largely confined to personal, single-task tools, lacking a team-level framework that fits the collaborative working patterns and multi-source data characteristics of the industry. To address this gap, this paper proposes an information-collaborative AI Agent framework oriented to real estate project teams: taking natural language as its unified interaction entry, the framework organizes project status, organizational rules, and business capabilities into a team-shared memory hub that is continuously updated as projects advance, supporting information collaboration and controlled execution among team members. The framework has been validated through practical business cases, effectively alleviating the difficulties of vast yet underutilized project management archives and of project experience anchored on individuals that is lost with personnel turnover, and providing a reference for the deployment of team-collaborative AI and the construction of system frameworks in real estate and related vertical industries.
【Keywords】Agent; Information Collaboration; Project Management; Knowledge Management; Large Language Models; Real‑estate Projects
